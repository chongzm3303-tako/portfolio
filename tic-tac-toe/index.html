Tic tac toe

  1. Take turns placing your mark (X or O) on an empty square.
  2. Get 3 of your marks in a line to win. The line can go across, down, or diagonally.
  3. You can only have 3 marks on the board. When you place a 4th, your oldest mark disappears.
  4. Your oldest mark looks faded, so you can see which one will disappear next.
